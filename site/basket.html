Quantities are not guesses. Each one is derived from the USDA Thrifty Food Plan's weekly pounds for a food category, divided among the items that represent that category in proportion to how much of each Americans actually buy (USDA ERS per-person availability), then converted into the unit the item is sold in. Where no public data divides a category, the split is even and says so below. The file is generated by a script, so it cannot drift from its sources.
