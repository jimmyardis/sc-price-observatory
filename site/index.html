Nobody measures grocery prices in South Carolina. We do.

BLS publishes no CPI for this state — the nearest official series lumps South Carolina in with the entire Census South. Every “South Carolina grocery cost” in circulation is a national number with a formula on top. This page reports prices collected from South Carolina stores, and the hours of average local work that buy a week of them.
